◈ Geographic Crime Intelligence

Four interactive views of recorded crime statistics

● MAPPING SYSTEM ONLINE

01. Crime Location Map

Displays recorded crime areas using individual location markers. Clicking a marker shows the area name and its recorded case count. Users can zoom in and explore different locations to understand where the available crime records are associated with.

02. Crime Heatmap

Uses different colour intensities to represent recorded crime counts across mapped areas. Brighter regions indicate higher recorded counts according to the selected data. This visualization helps users compare crime concentration and identify areas requiring further investigation.

03. Crime Cluster Map

Groups nearby mapped locations into clusters to make the map easier to understand. Each cluster displays a combined count of its represented records. Users can zoom in to separate individual locations and explore the distribution of recorded crime information across different parts of the region.

04. Crime Category Map

Displays crime information according to selected categories such as theft, robbery, assault and fraud. Different colours distinguish the selected category across mapped areas. Users can compare category-wise records and understand how recorded crime types vary between locations.
